# November 14, 2026: Made the 2 booms
<!-- fabricate:entry 2980 -->

I spent my entire day just on the model today so I am EXHAUSTED

I managed to figure out how to use revolute joints and rigid body groups to make the arm actually work decently, I still need to figure out how to make the actual grabber. The servo that I originally planned to use (MG996R) was waaay too big so I swapped it for a tiny MG90S, which means that I had to include lots of weight reduction, but hopefully it should work. I also made wiring channels inside the booms to keep everything tidy!
